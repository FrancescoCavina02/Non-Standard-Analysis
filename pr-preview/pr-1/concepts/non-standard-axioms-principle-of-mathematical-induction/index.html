Formal statement

(Principle of mathematical induction). Let P(n)P(n)P(n) be any
property pertaining to a natural number nnn. Suppose that P(0)P(0)P(0) is true,
and suppose that whenever P(n)P(n)P(n) is true, P(n+!+)P(\suc{n})P(n+!+) is also true. Then
P(n)P(n)P(n) is true for every natural number nnn.
